Whole Grain
Apple Nut Muffins

Servings: 12 | Amount Per Serving: 1 muffin

  • Calories: 150kcal | Fat: 5gr
  • Protein: 4gr | Carbohydrates: 30gr
  • Fiber: 4gr | Sugar: 19gr
  • Sodium: 170mg
  • Prep Time: 25mins
  • Cook Time: 35mins
  • Total Time: 1hr

Ingredients

  • 1) 1 cup apples, peeled, cored and grated
  • 2) 0.75 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 3) 1 teaspoon honey
  • 4) 0.5 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 5) Olive oil or butter
  • 6) 0.75 cup white whole wheat flour
  • 7) 0.75 cup coarse wheat bran flour
  • 8) 0.25 cup almond meal
  • 9) 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 10) 1/8 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 11) 0.25 teaspoon salt
  • 12) 1.5 tablespoons ground flaxseeds
  • 13) 1.5 cups homemade applesauce
  • 14) 0.75 cup brown sugar, not packed
  • 15) 1 tablespoon coconut oil
  • 16) 2 large egg whites
  • 17) 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 18) 28gr walnuts, chopped
  • 19) 28gr granola

Steps

  • 1) In a medium bowl, combine grated apple, 0.25 teaspoon cinnamon, honey, and lemon juice. Set aside.
  • 2) Preheat oven to 180°C. Line a muffin tin with liners and lightly drizzle them with olive oil or butter.
  • 3) In a medium bowl, combine flour, bran, almond meal, flax, baking soda, nutmeg, cinnamon, and salt with a wire whisk; set aside.
  • 4) In a large bowl mix applesauce, coconut oil, egg whites, sugar, and vanilla; beat at medium speed until thick. Scrape down sides of the bowl.
  • 5) Add the flour mixture, then blend at low speed until combined. Do not over mix. Fold in apples and walnuts.
  • 6) Pour batter into muffin tins and top each muffin with 0.5 teaspoon of granola.
  • 7) Bake in the center rack about 35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  • 8) Let them cool and serve room temperature.

Notes

  • 1) Substitute granola with a light sprinkle of oats or chopped nuts. Skip it entirely for a simpler muffin.
  • 2) Stir in a handful of raisins, dried cranberries, or chopped dates for extra sweetness.
  • 3) Keep the muffins in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 day, or refrigerate for up to a week.
  • 4) Freeze in a zip-top bag or container for up to 3 months. Let them thaw in the fridge overnight, or microwave straight from frozen in 20–30 second bursts until warm.
