Cottage Cheese Cheesecake

Servings: 12

  • Prep Time: 20mins
  • Cook Time: 40min
  • Cooling Time: 5hrs
  • Total Time: 6hrs

Ingredients

Graham Cracker Crust

  • 1) 4 tablespoons butter, unsalted, extremely soft (almost melted)
  • 2) 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 3) 1 cup graham cracker crumbs, slightly heaping
  • 4) 1 pinch salt (omit if using salted butter)

Filling

  • 1) 335gr cottage cheese
  • 2) 1 large egg
  • 3) 3 tablespoons butter, salted, melted and cooled
  • 4) 0.33 cup milk
  • 5) 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 6) 0.5 cup sugar
  • 7) 2.5 tablespoons flour
  • 8) 0.5 tablespoon lemon zest
  • 9) 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  • 1) Preheat oven to 180ºC. Grease an 8-inch springform pan.
  • 2) Combine softened butter, sugar, and a heaping half cup of graham cracker crumbs and press into bottom of the springform pan.
  • 3) In a blender or a food processor, process the cottage cheese until creamy.
  • 4) Add the egg and process for another 30 seconds, then add the melted butter, milk, and lemon juice.
  • 5) Stir together the sugar and flour and add to the mixture. Pulse to blend, then add the lemon zest and vanilla extract.
  • 6) Pour the mixture over the graham crust.
  • 7) Bake for 40 minutes. If using the springform pan, loosen the sides of the cake from the sides of the pan with knife but do not remove sides.
  • 8) Let cool at room temperature for about 40 minutes. Transfer to the refrigerator and chill for at least 5 hours (overnight is better) and serve.

Notes

  • 1) Lemon can be safely omitted.
  • 2) Crust can be removed and milk substituted for evaporated milk, but the composition then needs to change.
    Increase cottage cheese to 450gr, use 0.5 cup of evaporated milk, increase flour to 3 tablespoons, use 2 eggs, omit butter, and add a pinch of salt.
    The instructions remain the same.
