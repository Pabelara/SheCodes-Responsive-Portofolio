One Pan Creamy Lemon,
Feta & Zucchini Orzo

Servings: 8

  • Calories: 360kcal | Total Fat: 12g
  • Carbohydrates: 51g | Saturated Fat: 4g
  • Fiber: 3g | Protein: 14g
  • Sodium: 254mg | Sugar: 6g
  • Cholesterol: 19mg | Calcium: 159mg
  • Vitamin A: 2331IU | Vitamin C: 17mg
  • Iron: 1mg | Potassium: 450mg
  • Prep Time: 10mins
  • Cook Time: 10mins
  • Total Time: 20mins

Ingredients

  • 1) 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2) 0.5 sweet onion, diced
  • 3) 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4) 450g orzo pasta, uncooked
  • 5) 2 medium zucchini, grated
  • 6) 1 large lemon, zested + juiced
  • 7) 1 teaspoon Italian herb seasoning (oregano, basil, rosemary, thyme, sage)
  • 8) Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 9) Garlic and onion powder, to taste
  • 10) 3.5 cups low sodium chicken broth
  • 11) 1 cup milk
  • 12) 140g feta cheese, crumbled
  • 13) 2 tablespoons fresh dill, chopped

Steps

  • 1) In a large skillet, heat your olive oil on medium.
  • 2) Add in the onion and saute for 2 minutes.
  • 3) Add in the garlic and stir for 30 seconds.
  • 4) Add in the orzo and stir for one minute to coat.
  • 5) Toss in the zucchini shreds and stir for 2 minutes.
  • 6) Mix in the lemon juice, lemon zest, Italian herb seasoning, salt and pepper to taste, chicken broth and milk.
  • 7) Bring to a boil then lower the heat to a simmer and cook for 10-12 minutes or until the pasta is tender.
    If it needs more liquid, add some broth in to keep the texture creamy, not dry.
  • 8) When the pan is off the heat, stir in your crumbled feta and dill.
  • 9) To serve, top with a nice drizzle of olive oil.

Notes

  • 1) Substitute milk for evaporated milk for a creamier taste and texture.
  • 2) Consider adding peas, spinach and mushrooms for additional vegetables.
  • 3) Consider adding precooked chicken and/or chopped bacon for additional protein and flavor.
