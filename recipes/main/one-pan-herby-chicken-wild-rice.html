One Pan Herby
Chicken & Wild Rice

Servings: 4-5 | Serving Size: 1 chicken thigh with 1.25 cups rice and vegetables

  • Calories: 633kcal | Carbohydrates: 49g
  • Protein: 32g | Fat: 34g
  • Saturated Fat: 5g | Sodium: 780mg
  • Fiber: 6g | Sugar: 5.5g
  • Prep Time: 30mins
  • Cook Time: 1hr 15mins
  • Total Time: 1hr 45mins

Ingredients

  • 1) 800gr bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 4 to 5 thighs)
  • 2) 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 3) 1 tablespoon finely chopped fresh rosemary
  • 4) 2 teaspoons poultry seasoning
  • 5) 1.5 teaspoons kosher salt, divided
  • 6) 0.75 teaspoon black pepper, divided
  • 7) 0.5 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 8) 2 tablespoons butter
  • 9) 1 sweet onion, thinly sliced, approx. 2 cups sliced
  • 10) 3 ribs celery, thinly sliced on the bias
  • 11) 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 12) 3 large carrots, thinly sliced on the bias
  • 13) 2.5 cups chicken broth
  • 14) 1.5 cups dry wild rice blend
  • 15) 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
  • 16) 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves

Steps

  • 1) Preheat oven to 200°C.
  • 2) Place chicken in a bowl and toss with olive oil, herbs, seasoning, 1 teaspoon salt, 0.5 teaspoon black pepper, and ground cinnamon. Toss well to coat.
  • 3) Heat butter in a large oven-safe skillet over medium. Add chicken, skin side down, and cook, undisturbed, until golden-brown and crispy, about 6-7 minutes.
    Turn and cook 1 minute. Transfer to a plate.
  • 4) Add onion and carrots to pan (there should be enough residual fat from the chicken to cook the vegetables).
    Cook until softened, about 5 to 6 minutes or until onions are translucent.
  • 5) Stir in celery and garlic. Season with remaining salt and black pepper.
  • 6) Stir in wild rice and thyme; let rice toast for 1 to 2 minutes. Add broth and apple cider vinegar.
  • 7) Nestle chicken thighs back into pan (skin side up), cover, bring to a boil, and transfer pan to the oven.
  • 8) Cook for 35 minutes. Uncover and cook for an additional 15 to 20 minutes, until the rice is tender.
    Note: If you notice the rice start to dry out towards the end of cooking, add another splash of broth.
  • 9) Garnish with extra fresh herbs, if desired, and serve.

Notes

  • 1) Wild rice mix can be substituted by black rice or 100% wild rice, but adjust cooking times and liquid ratios.
    For black rice, reduce broth to 2.25 cups, bake covered for 25-30min and uncovered for 10-15min.
    For wild rice, increase broth to 4 cups, simmer the rice and vegetables for 20min first before moving the pan to the oven,
    then bake covered for 20min and uncovered for 10-15min.
  • 2) Poultry seasoning is a mixture of pepper, celery seed, thyme, sage, rosemary, and marjoram.
  • 3) If making in the instant pot, reduce the broth to 1.75 cups. Sauté chicken and vegetables as per the original recipe and toast the rice for a few minutes.
    Then deglaze the pot with the vinegar and broth, ensuring no brown bits are stuck on the bottom.
    Place the chicken skin side up on top of the rice. Pressure cook for 25min before allowing the instant pot to naturally release pressure over the next 10min.
  • 4) If using black rice when making in the instant pot, use 1.5 cups of broth and reduce the high pressure time to 20min.
    If using wild rice when making in the instant pot, use 2 cups of broth and maintain the 25min high pressure time.
