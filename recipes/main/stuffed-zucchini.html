Stuffed Zucchini

Servings: 2

  • Prep Time: 10mins
  • Cook Time: 30mins
  • Total Time: 40mins

Ingredients

  • 1) 2 medium zucchini
  • 2) 1 big onion, diced
  • 3) 1 can (90gr) of tuna
  • 4) Dash of soy sauce
  • 5) 2 teaspoons cornflour
  • 6) 1 cup milk
  • 7) Nutmeg, to taste
  • 8) Dried oregano and basil, to taste
  • 9 Onion and garlic powder, to taste
  • 10) 2 slices of Edam cheese
  • 11) 0.5 cup grated fresh Parmigiano ​Reggiano​
  • 12) Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 13) 2 tablespoons of olive oil

Steps

  • 1) Boil zucchini, 10 min in a pressure cooker or 20-25 min in an open pot.
  • 2) Slice boiled zucchini in half, scoop out and dice the pulp. Set aside the zucchini and let cool.
  • 3) In a large skillet, heat your olive oil on medium.
  • 4) Add in the onion and saute until translucent.
  • 5) Add the zucchini pulp and mix well.
  • 6) Add the tuna and stir for a few minutes.
  • 7) Add a dash of soy sauce and let simmer.
  • 8) Once cooked through, taste and add salt and pepper to taste.
  • 9) Stuff the cooled zucchini with the mix. Place in an oven safe dish and set aside.
  • 10) Preheat oven at 180ºC.
  • 11) While the oven warms, make the bechamel.
  • 12) In a medium saucepan, bring milk to a careful boil.
  • 13) Mix the cornstarch with a drizzle of milk in a separate bowl. Whisk together and, once combined, drizzle slowly into the boiling milk.
  • 14) Add nutmeg and a dash of salt to the boiling milk.
  • 15) Pour bechamel over the stuffed zucchini.
  • 16) Cover the zucchini with cheese.
  • 17) Grill for 5-10min until cheese has fuly melted.

Notes

  • 1) Substitute tuna for shrimp. Make sure to dice the shrimp before adding to the onion and zucchini mixture.
