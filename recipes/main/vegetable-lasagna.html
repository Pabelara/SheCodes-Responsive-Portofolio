Vegetable Lasagna

Servings: 4

  • Prep Time: 10mins
  • Cook Time: 40mins
  • Total Time: 50mins

Ingredients

  • 1) 6 precooked lasagna sheets
  • 2) 1 big onion, diced
  • 3) 4 garlic cloves, diced
  • 4) 1 medium carrot, shredded
  • 5) 90gr of mushrooms
  • 6) Half a medium zucchini, diced
  • 7) Half a medium eggplant, diced
  • 8) 390ml of tomato sauce
  • 9) 2 slices of chorizo asturiano
  • 10) 200gr of spinach
  • 11) 250gr of ricotta cheese
  • 12) 2 slices of Edam cheese
  • 13) 0.5 cup grated fresh Parmigiano ​Reggiano​
  • 14) Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 15) 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 16) Dried oregano and basil, to taste
  • 17) Onion and garlic powder, to taste

Steps

  • 1) Place precooked lasagna sheets in an oven safe dish to soften following box instructions.
  • 2) In a medium skillet, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil and saute the spinach until soft.
  • 3) In a large skillet, heat the remaining olive oil and chorizo on medium heat.
  • 4) Add the onion and oregano and saute until translucent.
  • 5) Add the garlic and stir for 2 minutes.
  • 6) Add the zucchini, eggplant and mushrooms.
  • 7) Add the carrots and saute until all vegetables are softened.
  • 8) Add the tomato sauce, salt, pepper, and remaining herbs and spices. Mix well.
  • 9) Mix spinach and ricotta to create a smooth, thick paste.
  • 10) Preheat oven at 180ºC.
  • 11) Remove the prepared pasta sheets from the oven safe dish and set aside.
  • 12) Add a thin base layer of tomato sauce to the container and place a layer of lasagna sheets on top.
  • 13) Cover the lasagna sheets with the ricotta and spinah mixture. Place another layer of lasagna sheets on top.
  • 14) Pour vegetable mix over lasagna sheets and cover again with more lasagna sheets.
  • 15) Cover top layer with tomato sauce, add Edam cheese, and sprinkle with grated cheese.
  • 16) Grill for 5-10min until cheese has fuly melted.
