Creamy Gnocchi Soup

Servings: 4

  • Calories: 617kcal | Carbohydrates: 77g
  • Fiber: 10g | Protein: 21g
  • Sodium: 1692mg | Sugar: 8g
  • Total Fat: 25g | Saturated Fat: 14g
  • Cholesterol: 70mg | Iron: 8mg
  • Potassium: 689mg | Calcium: 368mg
  • Vitamin A: 8031IU | Vitamin C: 20mg
  • Prep Time: 10mins
  • Cook Time: 20mins
  • Total Time: 30mins

Ingredients

  • 1) 2 carrots, diced
  • 2) 2 leeks, sliced
  • 3) 280gr mushrooms, chopped
  • 4) 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 5) 1 tablespoon dried provencal herbs mix, or use a mix of thyme, sage, marjoram and oregano
  • 6) 1 teaspoon dried tarragon
  • 7) 0.5 cup white wine
  • 8) 500gr dried gnocchi
  • 9) 3 cups vegetable stock or broth
  • 10) 0.75 cup heavy (double) cream
  • 11) 75gr pecorino, parmesan or Italian hard cheese, grated
  • 12) 2 handfuls fresh spinach, chopped
  • 13) 400gr pre-cooked chicken
  • 14) Onion Powder
  • 15) Garlic Powder
  • 16) Nutmeg
  • 17) 1 bay leaf

Steps

  • 1) In a medium–large stockpot or Dutch Oven, heat a layer of olive oil or a couple tablespoons of butter over a low heat alongside the bay leaf.
  • 2) Add the carrots and leeks and saute for a few minutes, until they are starting to soften and reduce.
  • 3) Add the mushrooms and continue to saute the veggies until the mushrooms have released all of their liquid and reduced in the size – about another five minutes.
  • 4) Add the dried herbs and garlic and saute for another minute or two, until fragrant.
  • 5) Now add the white wine to the pan and simmer for a couple of minutes.
  • 6) Add the chicken, vegetable stock, cream and gnocchi to the pan. Bring to a simmer, and cook for about 5-10 minutes until the gnocchi is soft.
    Note: If using half and half or single cream, don't add it now – save it for the next step. Lighter creams might split if they're added too soon.
  • 7) Stir in the cheese until it's melted through.
  • 8) Finally, add the spinach and simmer until it is wilted.
  • 9) Serve, with additional cheese for topping if you wish.

Notes

  • 1) Switch up the vegetables, onions, zucchini, peas, peppers, and tomatoes would all be great.
