Creamy Veggie,
Bean & Potato Soup

Servings: 4 large, 6 small | Amount Per Serving: 1.5 cups

  • Calories: 579kcal | Carbohydrates: 73g
  • Fiber: 16g | Protein: 27g
  • Sodium: 1172mg | Sugar: 8g
  • Total Fat: 22g | Saturated Fat: 13g
  • Cholesterol: 62mg | Iron: 9mg
  • Potassium: 1670mg | Calcium: 498mg
  • Vitamin A: 6828IU | Vitamin C: 73mg
  • Prep Time: 20mins
  • Cook Time: 30mins
  • Total Time: 50mins

Ingredients

  • 1) 1 medium red onion, diced
  • 2) 1 small carrot, diced
  • 3) 1 medium portobello mushroom, diced
  • 4) 1 medium sweet or bell pepper, diced
  • 5) 6 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 6) 2 teaspoons fennel seeds, lightly crushed with a pestle and mortar
  • 7) 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
  • 8) 1 tablespoon dried sage
  • 9) 1 tablespoon mixed Italian herbs (oregano, basil, rosemary, thyme)
  • 10) 1 cup (70 gr) spinach
  • 11) 2 cans beans (400gr), rinsed and drained
  • 12) 225 gr baby potatoes, quartered
  • 13) 4 cups (1 l) vegetable stock or broth
  • 14) 0.5 cup (120 ml) cream
  • 15) 1 cup (115 g) parmesan cheese
  • 16) 1 lemon, for juicing
  • 17) 1 handful fresh basil, chopped
  • 18) Onion Powder
  • 19) Garlic Powder
  • 20) Nutmeg
  • 21) 1 bay leaf

Steps

  • 1) Heat a few tablespoons of olive oil in a large soup pot alongside the bay leaf.
  • 2) Add the carrots, onions and pepper and saute for a few minutes until they are starting to soften.
  • 3) Add the mushrooms and saute until they are starting to release their liquid.
    If the pan feels too dry or sticky, you can add a splash of white wine or vegetable stock to loosen things up.
  • 4) Add the garlic, lemon zest and all of the dried herbs and spices. Mix through the pan until just fragrant.
  • 5) Add the potato, beans and vegetable stock.
  • 6) Bring to a boil, then turn down the heat and simmer for 20-25 minutes, until the potatoes are tender throughout.
    Taste test and adjust the seasoning as needed.
  • 7) Add the spinach. Stir through and cook for a minute or two until it's bright green and wilting.
  • 8) Add the basil, cream, cheese and juice of 1/2 of the lemon.
  • 9) Stir through until the cheese has melted through and a creamy soup has emerged!
  • 10) Serve with an additional squeeze of lemon on top if desired.
