Roasted Eggplant Soup

Servings: 6 | Amount Per Serving: 2 cups

  • Calories: 440kcal | Carbohydrates: 54g
  • Fiber: 13g | Protein: 15g
  • Sodium: 890mg | Sugar: 16g
  • Total Fat: 20g | Saturated Fat: 7g
  • Prep Time: 30mins
  • Cook Time: 30min
  • Total Time: 1hr

Ingredients

  • 1) 1 medium globe eggplant, cut into 1x1-inch cubes
  • 2) 4 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 3) 1.5 teaspoons salt, divided
  • 4) 1 yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 5) 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 6) 3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 7) 1.5 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 8) 1 teaspoon dried fennel seeds, finely chopped
  • 9) 0.5 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 10) 440g navy beans, rinsed and drainedk
  • 11) 4 cups stemmed and roughly chopped kale (lacinato or green curly)
  • 12) 710ml tomato puree
  • 13) 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 14) 4 cups water
  • 15) 0.5 teaspoon black pepper
  • 16) 2 heaping cups dry small pasta
  • 17) 1/3 cup grated Parmesan cheese plus more for garnish
  • 18) 0.5 cup heavy cream
  • 19) Optional topping: croutons

Steps

  • 1) Preheat oven to 220ºC. Spread eggplant on a rimmed baking sheet, and toss with 2 tablespoons oil and 0.5 teaspoon salt.
    Bake for 25 to 27 minutes, tossing once halfway through, until soft.
  • 2) Meanwhile, heat remaining 2 tablespoons oil in a Dutch oven or large soup pot over medium-high heat.
    Once hot, add the onion, and cook 6 to 7 minutes, until soft.
  • 3) Stir in the garlic, tomato paste, oregano, fennel seeds, and red pepper flakes.
    Cook for 4 to 5 minutes, until the tomato paste turns brick red.
  • 4) Add the beans, kale, tomato puree, vegetable broth, water, black pepper, and remaining teaspoon of salt; stir well until everything is combined.
  • 5) Bring the mixture to a boil. Once the soup is boiling, add the roasted eggplant and pasta.
  • 6) Reduce heat and simmer, uncovered, for 12 to 15 minutes, or until the pasta is al dente.
  • 7) Gradually sprinkle in the Parmesan cheese while stirring continuously.
  • 8) Once the cheese is combined, stir in the heavy cream, taste and adjust seasonings as needed, and serve.

Notes

  • 1) Substitute kale for spinach.
  • 2) Substitute navy beans for any other white beans like cannellini.
    Note that navy beans are small, round, and break down easily to create a rich, velvety texture.
    Cannellini beans are large, traditional kidney-shaped, and feature a thicker skin that allows them to stay intact during long simmering.
  • 3) Substitute cream for evaporated milk.
  • 4) Substitute vegetable broth for chicken or bone broth.
  • 5) In absence of tomato paste and tomato puree, grate fresh tomatoes for a similar effect.
  • 6) Consider adding other vegetables such as fresh mushrooms of roasted red pepper
